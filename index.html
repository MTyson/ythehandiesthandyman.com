Meet the Builder

thomas ray burkett jr. unincorporated

For years, Tom Burkett has been serving home buyers and homeowners across Southern Colorado. Known as the founder and lead inspector of **NOCO Home Inspection Detective**, Tom has inspected thousands of structures in the San Luis Valley, Saguache County, and beyond.

Through **The Handiest Handyman**, Tom takes that same level of diagnostic accuracy, engineering knowledge, and meticulous care and applies it directly to building, repairing, and home energy corrections. Whether it is diagnosing and correcting heat loss to keep homes warm in the winter, framing a custom cedar deck in Crestone, or repairing drywall in Salida, Tom is hands-on for every single project.
